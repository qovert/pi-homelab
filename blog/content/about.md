I work in IT at a university, which means I spend my days keeping other people's computers alive and my nights doing the same for my own — voluntarily, for fun.

The homelab is a cluster of Raspberry Pis running self-hosted services, with enough Ansible, containers, and cursed network config to keep things interesting. It's part learning environment, part practical infrastructure, and part excuse to buy more hardware.

When I'm not staring at terminal output, I'm soldering something together, automating something around the house, playing video games, or drinking beer — sometimes in combination.
